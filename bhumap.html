02 · Technical approach

Six parts, one workflow.

HARDWARE

RTK survey drone

A multirotor with RTK/PPK GNSS and a mapping camera. It flies at 80–100 m for a target ground resolution of 2–3 cm per pixel.

RTK / PPK80–100 m AGL2–3 cm/px
PROCESSING

Orthomosaic + DSM

Photogrammetry stitches the images into a geo-referenced map. Ground control points tie it to the national grid.

PhotogrammetryGCPsElevation model
HARDWARE

RTK rover

A GNSS receiver on a pole, corrected by CORS or a local base. It records boundary points to cm level.

CORS / baseTilt-compensatedcm-level
AI

Boundary extraction

A segmentation model (U-Net class) proposes bunds and field edges from the orthomosaic. A person reviews every one.

U-Net classHuman review
DATA

Unified land DB

A PostGIS parcel layer, linked by parcel ID to RoR, mutation and registration records through APIs.

PostGISParcel IDAPIs
APP

Offline-first app

An Android app for field verification. It stores everything locally and syncs when the network returns.

03 · Pipeline

Six steps from sky to record.

What happens to the data for every parcel, from the drone's first photo to a signed record in the land database.

01Drone imageryOverlapping photos from one sortie over the village.
02OrthomosaicStitched into one geo-referenced map with an elevation model.
03Rover control pointsCm-level RTK points at the corners the map can't settle.
04Parcel polygonsBoundaries drawn from the map and points, AI-proposed, human-checked.
05Owner verificationThe owner and the revenue official sign on the tablet, on site.
06Linked land recordThe parcel ID is tied to RoR, mutation and registration data.
04 · Feasibility

Built for a village with patchy signal.

The whole survey runs without a network. Data waits on the tablet and syncs to the district server when it can.

Field workflow

Village kick-off
A Gram Sabha notice goes out. Owners mark their boundaries with chalk or pegs.
Fly, then walk
One drone sortie covers the village. The rover team visits only disputed or unclear boundaries.
Confirm on site
The owner and the revenue official sign off on the tablet.

Field resilience

Works offline
All capture and verification runs without signal.
Auto sync
Data uploads to the district server once connected.
Quality checks
Rover points are compared with the drone map. Large mismatches are flagged for re-survey.

Software system

Officer dashboard
Tracks survey progress by village and parcel status.
Public map view
Owners can see their parcel and its record status.
Audit trail
Every edit is time-stamped and signed.

06 · Research

Built on what already works.

BhuMap brings together government practice, survey technology, field reality and our drone + rover hardware in one system.

Government programmes

SVAMITVA showed drone mapping of rural inhabited land. DILRMP targets digitised, linked land records. ULPIN gives each parcel a unique ID.

Survey technology

RTK/PPK GNSS and CORS networks give cm-level positioning. Photogrammetry produces orthomosaics and elevation models.
